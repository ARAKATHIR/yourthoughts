A few helpful answers

Questions, answered.

If you still have a question, message us before placing an order.

What do I receive?

A personalized mobile-friendly website and its QR code. The package determines the media limits, design level, included revisions and hosting period.

How do I share photos and videos?

After confirming the order, send your content directly to our team in WhatsApp. There is no public file upload form on this website.

Is the website created automatically?

No. Your Thoughts is a manually delivered service. A team member arranges your memories and creates the website.

When do I pay?

Pay 50% to begin. The remaining 50% is due after you approve the preview and before we send the final link and QR code.

How long does delivery take?

Allow up to 10 days after we receive your advance and complete content. We confirm the delivery date before starting; Premium orders receive priority.

How long does my website stay online?

Mini Memories is online for 7 days, Signature Memories for 30 days, and Premium Memories for 90 days from delivery.

Can anyone open the QR code?

Anyone with the web address or QR code can open the active website. It is not password protected, so please avoid sharing highly sensitive content.

What if I need extra content or more changes?

We’ll explain any extra cost and get your approval before doing work outside your package limits.
